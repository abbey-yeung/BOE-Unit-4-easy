Classroom Guide & Teacher Instructions

Overview & Curriculum Scope

This interactive assessment web app is custom-tailored for EDGE Unit 4 Text 2 (BOE). It provides a comprehensive 4-stage vocabulary progression designed to foster deep understanding, grammatical awareness, and contextual application of the 20 target items.

Four-Stage Progressive Mastery Breakdown

  • Stage 1 (Vocab 1–10): Displays exact English definitions in randomized order. Students type the English word and its part of speech (case-insensitive).
  • Stage 2 (Vocab 11–20): Displays exact English definitions in randomized order. Students type the English word and its part of speech (case-insensitive).
  • Stage 3 (Vocab 1–20): Mixed comprehensive exam covering all 20 vocabulary items in random order.
  • Stage 4 (Sentence Application): 120-item contextual sentence bank (6 unique sentences per word). Presented in batches of 20. Requires strict case-sensitive typing and proper inflected word forms (singular/plural, past tense, initial capitals).

Top-Right Classroom Student Lottery Tool

Anchored at the top-right of your screen, the lottery tool allows teachers to randomly select students to answer in class.

  • Default Roster: Preloaded with seat numbers No. 1 through No. 35.
  • Custom Names: Click Edit Roster to paste custom student names (one per line).
  • No-Repeat Mode: Prevents picking the same student twice during a classroom session.

Audio & Encouragement Engine

Positive English praise is dynamically randomized for correct answers to motivate students. Pleasant sound chimes accompany answers and lottery draws. The sound can be muted anytime using the top bar button.
